# AI Software Procurement Negotiator Feature Registry

- `saas-inventory` - SaaS Inventory (Spend): Products, owners, seats, contract value, renewal dates, and business criticality.
- `usage-shelfware` - Usage Shelfware (Optimization): Unused seats, low adoption, duplicate tools, inactive teams, and savings estimate.
- `renewal-calendar` - Renewal Calendar (Renewals): Notice dates, renewal deadlines, owner actions, and negotiation windows.
- `benchmark-pricing` - Benchmark Pricing (Negotiation): Price comparisons, discount bands, seat tiers, and leverage analysis.
- `negotiation-playbook` - Negotiation Playbook (Negotiation): Target concessions, fallback terms, procurement asks, and approval limits.
- `security-review-queue` - Security Review Queue (Risk): Security questionnaires, SOC reports, risk flags, and approval status.
- `budget-impact` - Budget Impact (Finance): Spend forecast, increases, committed savings, department allocation, and budget variance.
- `vendor-scorecards` - Vendor Scorecards (Vendor Management): Service quality, adoption, support, roadmap fit, and renewal recommendation.
- `approval-workflow` - Approval Workflow (Governance): Legal, security, finance, business owner approvals, and exception tracking.
- `executive-savings-report` - Executive Savings Report (Reporting): Savings pipeline, negotiated outcomes, risks, and next renewals.
- `documents` - Documents
- `notifications` - Notifications
- `integrations` - Integrations
- `profiles` - Profiles
- `ai-assistant` - AI Assistant
- `ai-tools` - AI Tools
